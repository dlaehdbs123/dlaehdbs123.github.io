Now

Researching efficient memory and compute architectures.

I am currently a Ph.D. student at Cornell Tech advised by Prof. Jae-sun Seo. My work focuses on DRAM design, emerging memory such as STT-MRAM, computing-in-memory, machine learning accelerators, and physical unclonable functions.

Selected Work

1T1C eDRAM Processing-In-Memory

Designed a micro sense amplifier for 1T1C embedded DRAM PIM, enabling multi-row access and improving throughput by 5.44X.

Related publication

DRAM IO Sense Amplifier

Developed an offset-cancel IO sense amplifier with static current-free pre-sensing, fabricated in Samsung 28nm CMOS.

Related publication

STT-MRAM Write Termination

Proposed a dual-mode inverter-based write termination and sense amplifier scheme for energy and area efficient STT-MRAM.
